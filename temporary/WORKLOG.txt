PERSISTENT PROJECT CONTEXT AND DECISIONS
----------------------------------------
- `agentic` is durable storage for reusable headless Arena tools and finished
  products. Arena runtime/workspace state is disposable.
- The durable toolbox is `tools/`; indexes are `tools/TOOLBOX.md` and
  `tools/toolbox.json`. Download only the selected entry at a confirmed ref.
- New products go in `WORK/` with a source/binary artifact, README, compact
  provenance, checksums, target status, and immutable raw GitHub link after
  publication. `products/` is legacy only.
- `TOOL_LOG.txt` is canonical; local `ARENA_LOG.txt` is the editable copy.
- Never commit Godot runtimes, SDK/JDK files, export templates, object caches,
  `.godot` cache, signing keys, passwords, tokens, or secrets.
